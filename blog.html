Keep a creative session note before chasing a phase label

Record the page name, its visible host, the sound goal, browser behaviour, and whether it asked for a download, notification, or account detail. Leave if the request does not fit a browser music experience. This creates a useful personal catalogue without inventing rankings. Continue with the Phase selection guide, mod safety guide, and focused Sprunki pages. Existing game/discovery links remain part of those pages; this blog does not use them to promote unrelated sites.
